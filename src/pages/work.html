Work

Creative ideas are at the heart of everything we do. A selection of launches, events and campaigns, with the brief, what we did and what it delivered.

Gatineau

Overnight trip at Beaverbrook

Brief

To host an overnight trip for press and creators, building on the cult status of the 2024 trip to Cliveden and further develop the Golden Glow franchise with the launch of Golden Glow Face Serum, as well as presenting 2025 NPD.

Activation

An overnight stay at Beaverbrook Hotel, Surrey. Guests were treated to a full brand immersion as well as trialling the new Golden Glow Face Serum and lab samples of the NPD for the rest of the year. Each room maximised social sharing with branding at every touch point. A padel session was filmed to create a Reel by Gatineau which the creators reshared. Drinks, dinner and breakfast the next morning created a strong relationship-building moment for the brand.

Results

Coverage secured from top influencers including Amanda Lamb, Rosie Green for YOU Magazine, Hannah Martin, Lisa Snowdon, Gen Turley and Really Ree. Editorial secured from Red, Woman & Home, YOU and Get The Gloss.

166 placements on Golden Glow Face Serum

KLIRA

Launching a new prescription skincare brand

Brief

Launch Dr Emma Craythorne’s new prescription skincare brand, KLIRA, to UK press, influencers and KOLs, securing high-profile coverage to cement KLIRA as the new leading brand in the category. Longer term, negotiate paid partnerships.

Activation

An interactive educational launch event at 180 Health Club, at which Dr Craythorne and team talked through the brand USPs and invited guests to sign up for their own prescription. Individual Zoom appointments for key press unable to make it. Ongoing stat-based pitches and profile pitching. Paid partnerships secured include Jo Jones and Nadine Baggott.

Results

Strong coverage across all major beauty news pages, skincare features, profile slots and beyond, leading to a very strong launch phase. Highlights included Style, Vogue, The Times, Elle, Red, Telegraph, Women’s Health and Stylist. Paid content proved very successful and long-term partnerships have been formed.

Vichy

Mineral 89 dinner parties

Brief

Introduce Vichy’s biggest launch of the year, Mineral 89 Eyes, to key press and influencers.

Activation

Top press and influencers were invited to host a Vichy Mineral 89 themed dinner party in the comfort of their own homes, to launch the new product and have their friends test it too. A private chef and waiters were on hand throughout the evening serving French-inspired food, with mineral-themed table dressings to highlight Vichy’s origins.

Results

The dinners featured widely across the hosts’ and guests’ social media pages, including Em Sheldon, Thuy Le, Kelly Eastwood and Vic Ceridono. Features in Grazia, Woman & Home, Vogue.co.uk, Sheerluxe.com, This Morning and The Times Magazine followed.

Total social reach over 1M

John Frieda Salons and Nicola Clarke Salon

Seasonal trend talks

Brief

Launch seasonal trends from the John Frieda and Nicola Clarke Salon.

Activation

Global celebrity colourist Nicola Clarke and Creative Director Zoe Irwin hosted seasonal Zoom trend presentations. These gained cult status for being informative, fun and often containing celebrity hair news not heard before, sparking story ideas.

Results

Each presentation was well attended by leading press, with those unable to attend requesting a recording. Features in Vogue.co.uk, marieclaire.co.uk, WhoWhatWear, Refinery29, Red online, harpersbazaar.co.uk, Elleuk.com, Red Magazine, Telegraph, Good Housekeeping, Sunday Times Style, Vogue, YOU Magazine, Observer Magazine and Evening Standard.

Element Eight

O2 Retinol Resurface Serum launch at Hide

Brief

Launch the new O2 Retinol Resurface Serum to key luxury press and influencers.

Activation

A breakfast and lunch session at Hide restaurant, London’s most sought-after breakfast destination, hosted by founder Gerard Camme to tell the Element Eight brand story, introduce the new serum and preview the wider 2026 NPD.

Results

The event built much stronger brand loyalty and converted a high calibre of press and influencers. Red awarded the serum a Best in Beauty award, and Katy Young, Beauty Director of Harper’s Bazaar, included Element Eight in her Best Skincare Discoveries of 2025.

33 pieces of content, reach of 1,635,034

Trepadora

Heat Protection Collection launch, ten years on

Brief

Launch Trepadora Curl Therapy’s most innovative launch to date as part of the brand’s tenth anniversary year: create its biggest moment yet and highlight the Babassu Thermal Care range of Quench Diffusing Serum and Quench Heat Protection Foam.

Activation

Curly creators and press were invited to a breakfast at 1 Warwick, Soho, hosted by founder Keisha Lawler, bringing the curly community together to meet Keisha, learn about the new range and celebrate the anniversary.

Results

Coverage from top influencers including Simone Powderly, Deb Falconer, Grace Middleton and Keisha Okwesia. Editorial in Red, Woman & Home, Stylist, Prima and Eliza. The launch marked the biggest moment for Trepadora and a step change in coverage and support.

Avène

Derm dinner

Brief

Build brand credibility and educate dermatologists around the key pillars, to help Avène become a go-to brand for their recommendations in clinic and beyond.

Activation

Key dermatologists were invited to an immersive dinner at The Brasserie of Light, Selfridges. Avène’s Head of Education and consultant dermatologist Dr BeiBei Du Harpur presented the key pillars and new launches, followed by a Q&A.

Results

Social support on the day, plus further quote opportunities via the derms who attended, resulting in additional meaningful mentions. Established relationships with industry KOLs helped ensure Avène has become a go-to brand for derms when prescribing products.

Ruby Hammer

Lip Serum Balm launch

Brief

Build excitement around the launch of the new Lip Serum Balms and position them as the hero of the range.

The Bronte Clinic

Launching the UK’s first female-centric health clinic

Brief

Launch the UK’s first female-centric health clinic, profiling the founders as leading experts in their field while raising awareness of the services on offer. Driving appointment bookings early in the campaign was crucial, alongside exploring corporate partnerships for ongoing educational initiatives.

Activation

Key press and influencers booked for one-to-one appointments in person and on Zoom, with specific pitching on certain services: menopause and sleep.

Results

Full-page features in The Times LUXX and ES Magazine. Filming with Trinny Woodall for her YouTube channel. Expert and clinic profiles and quotes in Vogue online, Metro, Style, Sheerluxe, Grazia Daily, Telegraph, Hello! and YOU Magazine.

Soapsmith

Six years of organic placement

Create a buzz for Soapsmith, highlighting the founder story and generating strong product placement in top-tier titles. All coverage organically placed.

Activation

Over six years we generated generous product send-outs accompanied by one-to-one appointments with the brand founder, and coordinated brand partnerships and sampling initiatives.

Results

Press highlights included Vogue, Harper’s Bazaar, Daily Mail, The Times, FT, YOU Magazine, ELLE Decoration, Red, Good Housekeeping, STYLE, Guardian Weekend, Grazia and more.

Little B by Bramley

A trusted British brand for children’s bath and body

Brief

Establish the Little B franchise firmly in the children’s bath and body space as a trusted British-born and manufactured brand, with excellent credibility and a strong founder story.

Activation

A combination of initiatives to build brand awareness, loyalty and engagement: product mailers, one-to-ones with founder Chloe Luxton, founder profiles, ongoing sampling and brand partnerships.

Results

Widespread coverage across national media and social media, with key pieces including Vogue and Hello! Beauty in print.
