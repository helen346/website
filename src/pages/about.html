About us

Based in London, Imagination is dedicated to growing the profile of beauty, wellbeing and lifestyle brands. Our clients range from some of the world’s most recognised beauty powerhouses to niche luxury and emerging brands.

Founder-led since 2009

Established in 2009, the founder-led team boasts over 50 years combined experience across a cross section of some of the world’s most prominent beauty and lifestyle brands.

With a constantly evolving industry, our approach demonstrates a perspective that seeks to combine exciting, forward-thinking strategies designed to elevate your brand, building reputation, curating community and delivering credibility and authority.

Our network incorporates a breadth of content creators, VIPs, KOLs, print media, digital media and broadcast. We personally know the people to make your brand stand out.

An extension of your business, we pride ourselves on providing a creative, nurturing, passionate team whose primary focus is on delivering a campaign that delivers optimal commercial successes, long term.

Helen Bruce, Founder
