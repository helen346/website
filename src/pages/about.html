About

Founder-led since 2009. Still answering the phone.

Imagination was set up to give beauty and lifestyle brands something the big agencies could not: the involvement of an in-house team, with the creativity and contacts of an agency.

The agency

Small on purpose

We are a dedicated PR agency specialising in strategic communications for the beauty, wellbeing and lifestyle sector. Since 2009 we have worked with brands ranging from some of the world's most recognised beauty houses to niche luxury and emerging names, delivering results through standout placement in print, feature pieces, strong digital content and influencer support.

Our founder-led team brings over fifty years of combined experience across a cross-section of the world's most prominent beauty and lifestyle brands. We stay deliberately small so that the people you meet are the people who do the work, and so that every client has a direct line to the founder.

As an extension of your business, we pride ourselves on being a creative, nurturing, passionate team whose primary focus is a campaign that delivers commercial success, long term.

Founder

Helen

Helen founded Imagination in 2009 after years inside beauty and lifestyle brands, and still leads every account. Clients describe the relationship as one that listens to their needs and works in a way that matches their brand ethos. Several have been with the agency for more than a decade; one has known Helen and her team for over fifteen years.
